# Kite Running Co. — store information

## Shipping
Free standard shipping on orders above ₹2,000 across India. Standard delivery takes 3–5 working days; metro cities usually receive orders in 2 days. Express delivery (next working day) is available in Mumbai, Pune, Bengaluru, Delhi NCR and Hyderabad for ₹199.

## Returns and exchanges
Unworn shoes can be returned or exchanged within 30 days of delivery. Keep the original box. Exchanges for a different size are free; refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5–7 working days after pickup.

## Sizing
Kite shoes run true to size. If you are between sizes, choose the larger size for running shoes and the smaller size for the Court One trainer. A printable size guide is available on the website.

## Demos and store visits
We run free fitting sessions and treadmill gait checks at our Pune (Koregaon Park) and Bengaluru (Indiranagar) stores. A demo can be booked through the assistant by sharing your name and email; the store team confirms a slot within one working day.

## Care
Machine-wash the Cloud Walk upper on a cold, gentle cycle. For other shoes, brush off dirt and wipe with a damp cloth. Do not tumble dry.
